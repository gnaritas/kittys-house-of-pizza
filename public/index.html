The station

A 1963 service station, back in service

Steve Garrahan and Kurt Drake — the founders of Bright Ice — are opening Kitty's House of Pizza in a renovated 1963 former gas station and convenience store at 4059 5th Ave N.

The team already runs Bright Ice ice cream shops in St. Petersburg and beyond. Now they're making pizza on the same stretch of 5th Avenue North — and yes, the pints travel too.

Good to know

Questions, answered

When does Kitty's House of Pizza open?
Tuesday, November 17, 2026.
Where is Kitty's House of Pizza?
At 4059 5th Ave N, St. Petersburg, FL 33713, in Central Oak Park — the restored 1963 service station on 5th Avenue North.
Is Kitty's takeout or dine-in?
Kitty's is a takeout-focused pizzeria — pies and pastas are made to be carried home.
Does Kitty's sell Bright Ice ice cream?
Yes. Bright Ice pints are sold at Kitty's — the pizza shop comes from Steve Garrahan and Kurt Drake, the founders of Bright Ice.
