Privacy

This page is short on purpose: here is everything this site does with information.

The contact form

When you send a note, we collect your name and message, plus your phone or email if you chose to leave them. The note is delivered to the Kitty's House of Pizza team and used for one thing: answering you.

The map

Directions and the embedded map are served by Google. When the map loads, Google may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy — this site doesn't read them.

No trackers, no ads

This site carries no analytics scripts, no advertising, and no cookies of its own. Nothing you type is shared or sold.

Questions

Want a note deleted or have a privacy question? Send it through the contact form and the team will handle it.

That's the whole policy.

A pizza shop with a website shouldn't need a scroll of fine print. It didn't.
