The menu board

A short menu, kept scratch.

Brick-oven pizza, house-made pastas, and pints of Bright Ice — that's the kitchen at Kitty's. The board starts the day the shop opens: Tuesday, November 17, 2026.

What's on

Three things, done properly

Brick-oven pizza

Scratch-made pies cooked in a brick oven and packed up hot for the road.

House-made pasta

House-made pastas cooked alongside the pies — the other half of the kitchen.

Bright Ice pints

Small-batch ice cream from the founders' Bright Ice shops, sold at Kitty's by the pint.
